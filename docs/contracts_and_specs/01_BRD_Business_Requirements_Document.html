Business Requirements Document (BRD)

Project: Direct-to-Consumer Cloud Kitchen & Catering Digital Platform for Raku's Kitchen

1. Executive Summary & Problem Statement

Raku's Kitchen is a premier home-cooked, authentic Nati-style food kitchen and catering service based out of Jambusavari Dinne, JP Nagar 8th Phase, Bengaluru. Operating primarily via an Instagram-driven audience (@rakus.kitchen), the kitchen experienced substantial operational friction handling orders manually over WhatsApp messages and direct phone calls (9606888096).

Core Challenges Identified:

  • Order Leakage & Miscommunication: Weight options (½ kg vs 1 kg vs multi-kg) and meat ratios (250g meat per ½ kg rice) were frequently misunderstood during chat conversations.
  • Cash Collection Inefficiencies: Cash-on-delivery resulted in high cancellation risks and delivery disputes; manual UPI screenshot verification took up to 15 minutes per customer.
  • Logistics Bottleneck: Copying customer addresses manually into Borzo or Porter created delivery dispatch errors and missed delivery cut-offs.
  • Slot & Capacity Overselling: With fixed batch preparations (ready at 12:30 PM for Lunch, 7:30 PM for Dinner, closed Mon/Thu, no Sunday dinner), manual tracking led to kitchen order overwhelm.

3. Business Process Transformation: AS-IS vs. TO-BE

AS-IS Process (Manual): Customer finds Instagram Reel → Sends WhatsApp message → Kitchen owner replies with menu text → Customer chooses items → Kitchen owner calculates price + packaging fee → Kitchen sends UPI QR → Customer transfers and uploads screenshot → Kitchen verifies bank balance → Kitchen copies address into delivery app → Frequent delivery and portion errors.
TO-BE Process (Automated via Nexus Solution): Customer clicks bio link → Selects Lunch/Dinner slot → Customizes Biryani weight & add-ons with automated constraints → Enters verified address via Ola Maps → Completes Razorpay digital transaction → System auto-syncs order to kitchen admin & WhatsApp → 1-Click Borzo courier dispatch from admin console → Real-time status tracker for customer.

5. Key Stakeholder Roles & Responsibilities

  • Kitchen Owner / Admin (Raku): Reviews live incoming orders, advances state (Confirmed → Preparing → Out for Delivery → Delivered), triggers Borzo couriers, and triggers automated WhatsApp customer notifications.
  • End Customer: Discovers dishes, customizes portions, enters verified delivery address via Ola Maps, prepays securely, and tracks live order status.
  • Nexus Development (Technology Partner): Full system engineering, infrastructure configuration, API integrations, ongoing bug-free warranty, and continuous menu/pricing maintenance under the ₹80,000 project agreement.
