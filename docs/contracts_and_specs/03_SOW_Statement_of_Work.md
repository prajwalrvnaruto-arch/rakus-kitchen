## 1. Contracting Parties & Agreement Summary

This Statement of Work ("SOW") establishes the formal agreement between **Nexus Development** ("Service Provider") and **Raku’s Kitchen** ("Client"). It codifies the deliverables built and deployed during the 2-month collaborative discovery and implementation period.

## 3. Infrastructure & Third-Party Accounts Ownership

- **100% Client Owned:** The Client (Raku's Kitchen) holds direct legal and financial ownership of all underlying third-party platforms:
  - Google Cloud / Firebase (Hosting, Firestore, Auth)
  - Razorpay Merchant Gateway (Payouts to Raku's Kitchen bank account)
  - Ola Maps API (Geocoding quota)
  - Borzo & Porter Courier accounts
- Nexus Development provides configuration and deployment services without holding client credentials or intermediate transaction balances.

---

## 4. Maintenance & Support Terms (Included in ₹80,000)

1. **Covered at Zero Additional Expense:**
   - 100% bug fixes and performance resolution for all delivered software code.
   - Ongoing minor maintenance: updating menu dish names, modifying prices, adjusting packaging fees, updating operating holidays, and revising notification text.
2. **New Feature Additions (Out of Scope):**
   - New feature modules beyond the agreed specification are billed at an hourly rate of **₹1,500/hour** (Time & Materials) subject to prior written approval.

---

## 5. Exclusions (Out of Scope)

- Native iOS/Android store submissions (App Store / Play Store).
- Third-party payment gateway transaction processing fees (Razorpay MDR).
- Courier delivery fees (paid directly by end customers to delivery drivers).
- Procuring kitchen hardware (tablets, printers, packaging).
