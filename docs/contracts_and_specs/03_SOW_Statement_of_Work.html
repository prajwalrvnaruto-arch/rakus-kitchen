3. Cloud Account & Infrastructure Ownership

In accordance with standard enterprise software governance, all third-party accounts, APIs, and cloud services are directly owned by and registered to the Client (Raku's Kitchen). The Client maintains direct billing relationships with all third-party vendors:

  • Google Cloud / Firebase: Firebase Auth, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Hosting/Storage.
  • Razorpay: Merchant account, KYC verification, and payout settlement bank account.
  • Ola Maps API: Map rendering and geocoding quota billings.
  • Fast2SMS Gateway: SMS credits, DLT registration and customer verification quota.
  • Borzo & Porter: Business delivery accounts and rider recharge wallets.

4. Maintenance, Warranty & Ongoing Support Terms

The total contract value of ₹55,000 includes a dedicated 1-Year Comprehensive Maintenance & Bug-Fix Warranty providing full 1-year warranty and ongoing maintenance support as follows:

  • Included Without Additional Expense (Under 1-Year Maintenance Warranty):
    • Full bug-fixing and technical support for all features and modules delivered under this SOW.
    • Ongoing minor operational changes including dish name changes, menu pricing updates, packaging fee adjustments, holiday hours changes, and operational banner updates.
  • Hourly Feature Expansion (Out of Scope):
    • Any major architectural additions or new feature modules (e.g., custom loyalty rewards, multi-branch kitchen support, native app builds) will be scoped separately and billed on a Time & Materials basis at ₹600/hour.

5. Boundaries & Exclusions (Out of Scope)

  • Native mobile application builds submitted to the Apple App Store or Google Play Store (the delivered solution is a Progressive Web App).
  • Payment gateway transaction charges (Razorpay standard MDR fees are settled directly between Razorpay and Client).
  • Doorstep delivery rider fees (courier costs are paid directly by end customers to Borzo/Porter delivery riders).
  • Kitchen food preparation hardware or physical tablet/printer hardware procurement.

6. Handover & Go-Live Schedule (20 Calendar Days Post-Signing)

Formal public Go-Live of the Raku's Kitchen Ordering & Delivery Platform is scheduled exactly 20 calendar days following the execution and digital signing of this document:

  • Days 1–7 (Setup & Verification): Live payment gateway settlement validation, kitchen staff portal training, and final menu catalog verification.
  • Days 8–14 (Operational Drills & Logistics): Real-world test dispatches with Borzo & Porter delivery riders and simulated kitchen lunch/dinner peak volumes.
  • Days 15–20 (Launch Readiness): Customer WhatsApp bio-link routing, QR collateral distribution, and final pre-flight health checks.
  • Day 20: Official Public Go-Live and transition into 1-Year maintenance warranty support.
